about me

Always enjoyed building stuff and learning new things, I guess that's why I'm drawn to startups and entrepreneurship.

I love reading, cooking, and traveling. I also enjoy spending time with my loved ones and my dog, especially out in nature. Occasionally surfing… but honestly, not very good at it.
